## Overview
The Central Library (HariNarayan Dutta Baruah Central Library), Assam down town University was established in the year 2010 with the main motto of fulfilling the essential knowledge needs of the students, faculties, and research scholars and staff of the university. The Central Library functions with great precision and dedication to promote academic excellence.
##### Library Location
The Central Library is located in the “K Block” named as Bornadi which is near to the temple area in the university campus. The Library is spread over four floors of the building.

The HNDB Central Library features an extensive array of specialized collections catering to diverse interests. The Competitive Section offers vital resources for exam preparation, while the Mythology Section preserves ancient religious texts like the Vedas and the Quran. The Children Section fosters a love for reading among young readers, and the Music Section serves as a haven for music enthusiasts and researchers. The Banga-Bandhu Section, a gift from Bangladesh, includes international books and photographs. The Hari Narayan Dutta Baruah Section houses works by the celebrated author. The library also offers a vast collection of Sanskrit texts, the Pt. Deendayal Upadhyay collections, and over 100 rare books spanning various subjects. Unique collections feature local historical artifacts and vintage records. The Manuscript Section contains digitized Chitra Bhagawata and traditional manuscripts. The Braille Section supports visually impaired readers, while the Alumni Section fosters lifelong learning and camaraderie among alumni.
![](https://adtu.in/hndb-library/assets/images/ilms.jpeg)
PRAN is an institutional library management software (ILMS) of Assam down town University. It streamlines library operations, encompassing cataloging, circulation, acquisition, and serial management, ensuring efficient and organized library workflows. The software integrates user-friendly interfaces and robust search functionalities, allowing users to easily access and manage a vast collection of academic resources. By leveraging modern technology, PRAN significantly enhances the overall library experience, facilitating improved resource management and accessibility for the university's academic community.
